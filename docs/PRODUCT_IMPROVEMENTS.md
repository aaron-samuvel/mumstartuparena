# Product improvements

Ideas are evaluated for usefulness, feasibility, data availability, cost, security and user experience. Items remain proposals until their data and safety requirements are satisfied.

## Source confidence and freshness labels

- **Usefulness:** High. People can see whether a listing is current and how it was checked.
- **Feasibility:** High; supported by the source and verification timestamps in the data model.
- **Data:** Requires a successful source check or manual reviewer action.
- **Cost/security/UX:** Low cost; explain stale and pending states clearly without implying verification.
- **Decision:** Included in the foundation; show verification state in discovery surfaces before launch.

## Coverage transparency

- **Usefulness:** High. An empty neighbourhood should not look like a zero-activity market.
- **Feasibility:** High; report the date range and source coverage beside every statistic.
- **Data:** Depends on source coverage by locality and time period.
- **Cost/security/UX:** Low cost; avoid publishing analytics when samples are too small.
- **Decision:** Include in analytics acceptance criteria.

## Candidate privacy controls

- **Usefulness:** High for resume-based matching.
- **Feasibility:** Moderate; needs retention/deletion, consent, access control and storage lifecycle.
- **Data:** Candidate-provided resume and profile only.
- **Cost/security/UX:** Moderate implementation cost; sensitive personal data needs strict protection.
- **Decision:** Required before enabling resume upload or AI career assistance.
